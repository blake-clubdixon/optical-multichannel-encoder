# optical-multichannel-encoder

## Overview

An experimental optical multi-channel encoding system inspired by the operation of the Enigma Machine (circa 1918).

The project explores transmission, detection, and processing of parallel optical signals through polymer optical fiber (POF) using photodiodes, analog signal conditioning, and digital signal recovery.

## Project Goals

- Develop a 26-channel optical transmission system.
- Investigate optical power loss through the coupling chains.
- Design a photodiode receiver.
- Convert optical signals into electrical signals.
- Integrate the optical, electrical, and mechanical subsystems together.

## Current Status

The optical receiver module, rotor assembly, power supply board, and lateral displacement couplings have been developed and are in the initial stages of prototyping. 

## Future Work

- Improve coupling efficiency and fiber termination to reduce optical power loss.
- Test and characterize receiver sensitivity.
- Scale single-channel tests to larger multi-channel tests.
- Integrate encoding rotors and pawl mechanisms.
- Design a custom PCB
